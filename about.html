About the Event

Raas Rang

Raas Rang — Dandiya Night is a festive school-hosted event presented by Vanasthali Public School, Vasundhara Sector-3, Ghaziabad.

The event is designed as an evening of Dandiya, Garba, music, food, games, attractive stalls, prizes and celebration.

Date 14 October 2026
Time 6 PM – 10 PM
Female Entry ₹150
Couple Entry ₹300

Event Highlights

  • Garba and Dandiya celebration
  • Food and refreshment stalls
  • Games and activities
  • Attractive stalls
  • Prizes and entertainment
  • Dance and music

Entry Categories

The event provides two registration categories:

  • Single Female Entry: ₹150
  • Couple Entry: ₹300 per couple

No stag entry is permitted.

Online registration creates an event entry record only after successful payment verification by the payment gateway and registration system.
