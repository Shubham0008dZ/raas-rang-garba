Data Protection

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected for Raas Rang Dandiya Night registration, why it is collected and how it should be protected.

Information we collect

  • Lead guest name, mobile number and email address.
  • Names of other registered guests.
  • A live selfie of the lead guest when required.
  • Registration, payment-reference and gate check-in information.

Why we collect it

  • To create and manage event registrations.
  • To send registration/payment/entry communications.
  • To verify entry and prevent duplicate check-in.
  • To reconcile payments and resolve support issues.

Payment information

Payment credentials are processed by the payment gateway. The event website must not store card number, CVV, PIN or OTP.

Selfie and security

The live selfie should be transmitted over HTTPS to secured access-controlled storage and used only for the published event verification purpose. The production system should enforce a limited retention period based on the event's actual need.

Third parties

The payment gateway and hosting/storage providers may process limited information required to operate the registration service.

Your requests

For correction, privacy or deletion questions, contact the support address on the Contact Us page. Requests may be limited where records must be retained for legitimate legal, payment or fraud-prevention reasons.
