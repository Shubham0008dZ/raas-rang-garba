Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how registration information collected through the Raas Rang online registration website is used and protected.

The website is operated for registration and event-management purposes for Raas Rang — Dandiya Night at Vanasthali Public School, Sector-3, Vasundhara, Ghaziabad.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the registration flow, we may collect:

  • Lead guest name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Names of accompanying guests
  • Registration and payment references
  • Live selfie captured during registration
  • Technical information required for security and fraud prevention

2. Why We Collect This Information

  • To create and manage event registrations
  • To communicate registration or event information
  • To process and reconcile payments
  • To verify registration at the event entry gate
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate registrations and misuse
  • To respond to customer support requests
  • To maintain necessary event and transaction records

3. Payment Information

Online payments are processed through the payment gateway provided during checkout.

The website does not ask users to enter or send their card security credentials, payment gateway secret keys or banking credentials directly to the website.

Payment processing and related payment information are handled by the payment service provider according to its applicable policies.

4. Live Selfie

The registration flow may request a fresh live selfie from the lead guest for event registration and entry-verification purposes.

The selfie is intended to be used only for the purposes described during registration, subject to the event's operational and retention requirements.

The selfie is not intended to be placed in a public registration page or publicly accessible gallery.

5. Information Sharing

Registration information may be shared only where reasonably necessary for event administration, payment processing, security, customer support, legal compliance or service delivery.

Customer card details are not intentionally collected or stored by this website.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used to protect registration information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or misuse.

Access to sensitive information is restricted to authorised systems and personnel where required for event operations.

7. Data Retention

Registration and transaction information may be retained for the period necessary for event administration, reconciliation, customer support, security, accounting and applicable legal or compliance requirements.

Sensitive information that is no longer required should be deleted or securely disposed of in accordance with the applicable retention requirements and the site's operational policy.
