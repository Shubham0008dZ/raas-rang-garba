Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through the Raas Rang registration website may be collected, used, stored and protected for event administration.

1. Information We Collect

  • Lead guest name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Names of accompanying guests
  • Registration information
  • Payment references and transaction identifiers
  • Live selfie captured during the registration flow
  • Technical information required for security and fraud prevention

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage event registrations
  • To communicate important registration or event information
  • To reconcile and verify payments
  • To verify entry at the event venue
  • To prevent duplicate registrations and misuse
  • To respond to customer support requests
  • To meet necessary operational or legal requirements

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed through the payment gateway presented during checkout.

This website is not intended to collect or store card security credentials, payment gateway secret keys or banking credentials directly from customers.

4. Live Selfie

The registration process may request a fresh live selfie for registration and event entry-verification purposes.

The selfie is intended for the event-related purposes disclosed in the registration flow and should not be treated as public website content.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with authorised service providers, payment-processing systems, event staff or other parties only where reasonably necessary for registration, payment processing, security, customer support, legal compliance or event administration.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect registration information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or misuse.

7. Data Retention

Registration and transaction information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for event administration, payment reconciliation, customer support, security, accounting and applicable legal or compliance requirements.
