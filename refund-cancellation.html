Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund & Cancellation

This policy applies to online registrations and entry payments for Raas Rang — Dandiya Night.

Event Ticket Cancellation

Once an event registration payment has been successfully completed and the registration has been confirmed, customer- initiated cancellation is generally not permitted and the event entry fee is non-refundable.

1. Duplicate Payment

If the same customer is charged more than once for the same registration because of a duplicate transaction, the duplicate amount may be reviewed and refunded after verification.

2. Failed or Unsuccessful Payment

If a payment is not successfully captured, no confirmed event registration will be issued against that unsuccessful payment.

If the customer's bank account is debited but the payment remains unsuccessful at the registration system, the transaction will be reviewed against the payment gateway records.

3. Event Cancellation by Organiser

If the event is cancelled by the organiser and the organiser announces that ticket payments will be refunded, eligible payments will be refunded to the original payment method used for the transaction.

4. Event Rescheduling

If the event is rescheduled, the organiser may announce whether existing confirmed registrations will remain valid for the revised date or whether a refund window will be provided.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be initiated through the applicable payment channel and processed according to the payment service provider's settlement and banking timelines.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the customer's bank or payment method.

6. Refund Request

For an eligible refund request, customers should contact the event support team using the details below and provide the registration number and payment reference, where available.

Email: vpserpvasundhara@gmail.com

Phone: 0120-4111062 / 011-22456133

7. No Unauthorised Refunds

Refunds will not be issued merely because a customer changes their mind, does not attend the event, arrives after entry procedures close, or otherwise chooses not to use a confirmed ticket, except where the organiser's announced policy provides otherwise.

8. Policy Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this website before it is applied to future transactions, where applicable.

Important: This policy is intended for the Raas Rang event and should be published only after it has been approved by the event organiser / school authority.
