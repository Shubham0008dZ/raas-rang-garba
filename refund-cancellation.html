Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund & Cancellation

This policy applies to online registration and entry payments made for Raas Rang — Dandiya Night.

Confirmed event registrations are generally non-refundable once payment has been successfully completed.

1. Customer-Initiated Cancellation

Once an online registration has been successfully paid for and confirmed, customer-initiated cancellation is generally not permitted and the entry fee is non-refundable.

2. Duplicate Payment

Where a customer is charged more than once for the same registration due to a duplicate transaction, the duplicate transaction may be reviewed and refunded after verification.

3. Failed Payment

If a payment is not successfully captured, no confirmed event registration will be issued against that unsuccessful payment.

Where a customer's bank account has been debited but the payment is not reflected as successfully captured by the payment system, the transaction may be reviewed against the available payment records.

4. Event Cancellation by Organiser

If the organiser cancels the event and announces that eligible ticket payments will be refunded, such refunds will be processed to the original payment method wherever technically possible.

5. Event Rescheduling

If the event is rescheduled, the organiser may announce whether existing registrations remain valid for the revised date or whether an eligible refund window will be provided.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be initiated through the applicable payment channel. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment method and banking system.

7. Non-Attendance

A confirmed entry fee is generally not refundable merely because a customer does not attend the event or chooses not to use the registration.

8. Refund Request / Support

For an eligible refund-related query, contact the event support team and provide the registration number and payment reference wherever available.

9. Policy Updates

Any material change to this policy will be reflected on this page for future transactions, where applicable.

10. Important

This policy must reflect the actual refund and cancellation rules approved by the event organiser. The organiser's announced policy will govern eligible refund decisions.
